# Archaeology of the Ancient Mediterranean Major | U-M LSA U-M College of LSA

## Extracted Requirements Section

Requirements Minimum Credits: 27 Requires a minimum of 8 courses (at least 3 credits each) including:two of the following introductory courses: ARCHAM 221, 222, or 228one broad introductory course in either Greek or Roman history or civilization (200-level or above), such as CLCIV 202, 302; HISTORY 200, 201three upper-level courses (numbered 325 and above) in the Archaeology of the Ancient Mediterranean (ARCHAM)one course in Ancient Greek or Latin that helps the student achieve third-term proficiency (usually met by successful completion of GREEK 301 or the equivalent, or LATIN 231 or the equivalent). Students who plan to fulfill this requirement in other ways should speak to the department advisor.At least one additional relevant course at the 400-level (ARCHAM 499 may be substituted only with prior approval of the advisor)Students interested in possibly continuing in the field should discuss their plans with the undergraduate advisor as early and as frequently as possible. Graduate school in Archaeology of the Ancient Mediterranean requires knowledge of both Ancient Greek and Latin and the department advisor can assist in planning a curriculum to include both languages. Other Department Policies Field ExperienceRecommended but not required for a major in Archaeology of the Ancient Mediterranean There are several opportunities for students to join excavations in the Mediterranean area under the supervision of University of Michigan faculty. Contact the Department to speak with an advisor.

Grade Policies The department requires that a student earn a grade of at least C– in all language courses which are prerequisite for subsequent elections. A student should repeat any language course in which a D+ or lower grade is earned and which serves as a prerequisite to other courses which are to be elected. A grade of D+ signifies some achievement but denotes too weak a foundation for subsequent courses. Prerequisites None.
