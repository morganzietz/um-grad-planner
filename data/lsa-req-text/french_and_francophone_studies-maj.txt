# French and Francophone Studies Major | U-M LSA U-M College of LSA

## Extracted Requirements Section

Requirements Minimum Credits: 30 A minimum of 30 credits in FRENCH courses beyond the prerequisite, including:FRENCH 235 or RCLANG 320A minimum of two FRENCH 270-299 coursesAt least 18 credits of FRENCH courses 300-level or aboveMajors must take one of the following literature and culture courses in RLL at the Ann Arbor campus: FRENCH 350-379A maximum of two courses in the major may be chosen from French courses taught in English without language prerequisites. These courses include (but are not limited to): FRENCH 240-249, 340-349, 440-449.Students who begin their French coursework in the language program sequence (FRENCH 100-232) may replace one 240-level French course taught in English with three credits from FRENCH 232, FRENCH 230, or RCLANG 290.FRENCH 250x AP or IB credit may count as one FRENCH 270-299 course taught in French. For more details, visit: lsa.umich.edu/rll/undergraduates/language-placement/ap-and-ib-credits.htmlStudents may substitute RCLANG 320 for FRENCH 235. Students who complete a second and/or a third RCLANG 320 course will receive major credit for a French elective at the 270-299 level.French majors are encouraged to consider the possibility of studying abroad. Constraints To ensure that French majors gain a broad range of knowledge in the French language, literature, and culture, a maximum of six credits from the French professional and/or internship courses (FRENCH 278, 281, and 381) as well as any of their equivalents accepted as transfer and/or study abroad credit can count toward the major or minor.
