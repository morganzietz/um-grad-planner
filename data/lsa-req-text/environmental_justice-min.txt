## Extracted Requirements Section

Requirements Minimum Credits: 15 Foundational Course Requirement (choose one): ENVIRON 222: Introduction to Environmental JusticeENVIRON 242: Topics in Environmental Social Science with the topic, "Indigenous Environmental Justice"Students may use the course not taken to meet the Foundational Course Requirement as one of their Interdisciplinary Electives courses. Interdisciplinary Electives (at least 12 credits): Students elect interdisciplinary courses from a pre-approved course list that address environmental inequities from a disciplinary perspective (e.g. social sciences, humanities, geography) or a topical approach (e.g. water, land use, climate change). At least 6 credits must be 300-level or above.

Advising Environmental Justice minor advising will be overseen by the Assistant Director of Advising and Student Life in PitE. PitE offers bi-weekly information sessions for students to become familiar with our major and minors. Prospective students may attend those or our open Peer Advising times to learn about and declare our major and all minors. Declared minors can be directly seen by two to three staff advisors that work in PitE.
