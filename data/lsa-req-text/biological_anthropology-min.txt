Biological Anthropology Minor (through Summer 2014) The minor in Biological Anthropology allows students with interests in evolutionary biology or medicine to add an anthropological perspective to their studies. Many students are interested in specific topics covered by Biological Anthropology, such as Human Evolution, the Evolution of Human Behavior, Primatology, Nutrition and Adaptation, Human Genetics, etc. Thus, a minor in biological anthropology offers several "tracks" to students depending on their interests. An academic minor in Biological Anthropology is not open to students with any major or any other minor in the department of Anthropology. Effective through Summer 2014 Prerequisites to the Minor None.
