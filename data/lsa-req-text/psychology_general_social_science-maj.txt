# Psychology (General Social Science) Major | U-M LSA U-M College of LSA

## Extracted Requirements Section

Requirements Minimum Credits: 32 A minimum of 32 credits, excluding the prerequisites and courses within the major.1. Breadth Requirement: One course from each of three breadth groups Group I: PSYCH 220 or 235 or 240Group II: PSYCH 250 or 270Group III: PSYCH 280 or 290 or 2912. Lab Requirement: To meet the lab requirement, students may complete either:two (at least 3 credits each) courses from the list of Methods-based Lab courses;one (at least 3 credits) course from the list of Methods-based Lab courses and one (at least 3 credits) course from the list of Experiential Lab courses (i.e., Community-based or Research-based); orthe Psychology Thesis Research sequence (at least 6 credits total) A total of six credits of Psychology letter-graded Experiential lab (community or research-based courses) may be counted towards the Psychology major. A minimum of three credits of Research-based courses must be taken in a single academic term to satisfy the Psychology Lab requirement. Students wishing to pursue the Psychology Honors Research Major should acquire research and statistical skills early in their major before applying.See the Psychology Department's Course Information page for a list of courses that fulfill this requirement.3. Electives: At least 4 upper-level electives (lecture/seminar-based) for the major, totaling at least 12 credits:at least two of the courses must be at the 300-level (PSYCH 225 or COGSCI 200 may be substituted for one of these courses)at least one of the courses must be at the 400-levelSee the Psychology Department's Course Information page for a list of courses that fulfill this requirement.4. Additional elective, if needed to achieve 32 major credits: If a student completes all the above coursework but has not reached 32 credits, they must complete additional Psychology coursework until 32 credits are achieved, which can include:All PSYCH courses at the 300-level or above, and/orOne of PSYCH 211, 220, 226, 235, 240, 250, 270, 280, 290 or 291If a student has used a course to meet the Breadth Requirement for the major, it cannot then be used to meet the Additional elective. Constraints Student double majoring in Psychology and Neuroscience may share a maximum of 3 courses toward their two programs.
