# Scandinavian Studies Minor | U-M LSA U-M College of LSA

## Extracted Requirements Section

Requirements Minimum Credits: 15 At least 15 credits and five courses, to be chosen from the following categories as stated below.Required Scandinavian courses: SCAND 325, 331, and 375.Electives: Two courses chosen from among:SCAND 351, 421, 430, 442, 460, 480.Appropriate (Scandinavian topic) sections of: ENGLISH 407, FTVM/ENGLISH 330, and FTVM 441.PHIL 371. Scandinavian Studies Minor (Fall 2012-Fall 2013) Scandinavian Studies Academic Minor Effective Fall 2012-Fall 2013 Not open to student electing any minor in the Department of Germanic Languages and Literatures Prerequisites to the Minor SCAND 234 or equivalent, as determined by the Departmental placement examination. Requirements for the Minor At least 15 credits and five courses, to be chosen from the following categories as stated below. Required Scandinavian courses: SCAND 325, 331, and 375. Electives: Two courses chosen from among: SCAND 351, 421, 430, 442, 460, 480. Appropriate (Scandinavian topic) sections of: ENGLISH 407, SAC 330, and SAC 441. PHIL 371.
