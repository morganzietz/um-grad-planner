Advising Department Website Effective Winter 2016 Advising The Global Islamic Studies Center offers an interdisciplinary academic minor to students who wish to better understand the history and culture of the Islamic religion and Muslim societies and to gain insight into the political, economic, and social transitions taking place in this field.International Institute (II) undergraduate academic advisors will assist students in developing a program of study. Students are encouraged to schedule an advising appointment with an II undergraduate advisor to discuss prerequisite courses, requirements, declaring, academic planning, course selection, registration, senior releases, and other matters of concern. For more information, visit the II Advising page. Prerequisites None.
