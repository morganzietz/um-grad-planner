Requirements Minimum Credits: 15 One AMCULT course (3 credit) at 200-level, or higher (AMCULT 222 and 223 are excluded).AMCULT 300: Approaches to American Culture (3 credits).Three additional courses (9 credits total) at 300-level or higher.
