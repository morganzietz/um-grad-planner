# Social Theory and Practice Major | U-M LSA U-M College of LSA

## Extracted Requirements Section

Requirements Minimum Credits: 30 A requirements checklist for the STP Major is available on the Residential College's website.All students take an RCSTP Theory course and a Research Methods course: RCSTP Theory Course (choose one):RCSTP 301: Social Theory for Social ChangeRCSTP 302: Contemporary Social and Cultural TheoryResearch Methods Course (choose one; options include but are not limited to):STATS 250: Introduction to Statistics and Data AnalysisQMSS 201: Intro to Quantitative Methods in the Social SciencesSOC 210: Introduction to Statistics for Social ScienceSOC 310: Sociological Research MethodsAdditionally, students complete a minimum of 18 credits – at least six upper-level courses (300-level or above) – exploring and informing the student’s self-identified social justice interests, questions, approaches, theories, and practices. These courses are selected in RCSTP 290, but can also be taken before a student takes 290 and can be modified in conversation with the STP advisor as the student progresses through the major. These 18 credits include:A second social theory course (at least 3 credits)A community engaged-learning/practicum course (at least 3 credits)Common selections include classes in the Prison Creative Arts Program (PCAP), the Spanish Language Internships Program (SLIP), Telling It, PALMA, Semester in Detroit (SiD), Project Community in Sociology, and Project Outreach in Psychology.At least 4 electivesStudents enroll in a capstone in the fall of their senior year: RCSTP 490: STP Capstone - Senior Project SeminarAll STP students complete a self-designed Senior Project within RCSTP 490. These projects can take many forms, including a traditional, 25-35 page research paper; a shorter paper analyzing and reflecting on justice-focused community-engaged work; and/or creative projects such as performances, installations, documentaries, etc., accompanied by research-informed write-ups.
