## Extracted Requirements Section

Requirements Minimum Credits: 35 Once a student has mastered introductory content and declared the major, they prepare for advanced study of the discipline by fulfilling the Intermediate Courses Requirement (three courses), then move on to master advanced concepts, skills, problems, and topics of the discipline by completing the Advanced Courses Requirement (five courses), and Elective Requirement (four courses).Intermediate Courses RequirementStudents must complete all of the following:PHYSICS 316: Mathematical Methods for Physics & EngineeringPHYSICS 390: Introduction to Modern Physics PHYSICS 391: Introduction to Modern Physics Lab Ideally, PHYSICS 316 is elected no later than the second semester of the sophomore year. PHYSICS 390 and 391 can be elected concurrently with PHYSICS 401 or 405 (part of the Advanced Courses Requirement).Advanced Courses RequirementStudents must complete all of the following:PHYSICS 401: Intermediate MechanicsPHYSICS 405: Intermediate Electricity and MagnetismPHYSICS 406: Statistical and Thermal PhysicsPHYSICS 441: Advanced Laboratory IPHYSICS 453: Quantum MechanicsStudents are encouraged to complete both PHYSICS 401 and 405 before electing PHYSICS 453.Elective RequirementStudents round out their study of physics by taking a minimum of four elective courses (totaling at least twelve credits) of the choices listed below. Note that any course used in the Intermediate and/or Advanced Course requirements cannot also fulfill an elective.PHYSICS 340: Waves, Heat, and LightPHYSICS 360: Honors Physics IIIPHYSICS 380: Intermediate Topics in Physics, section titled "Introduction to Quantum Computing" (Note: other topics taught as PHYSICS 380 are not automatically included in the major; see an advisor)CMPLXSYS 335: NetworksAny PHYSICS course numbered at the 400- or 500-level, excluding PHYSICS 501, 517, 518 and 590 Constraints Courses elected as pass/fail may not be included in the major.Students may use a maximum of three credits of independent study, such as PHYSICS 415, 495, 496, or 497, towards the major requirements. This includes students pursuing an honors major. Honors majors should not elect PHYSICS 495 as it does not fulfill any requirements for the honors major.
